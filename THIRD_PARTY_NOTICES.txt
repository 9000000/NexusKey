2. vkey_engine.dll  --  only if present in this archive
================================================================================

Some releases publish an optional Vietnamese processing engine,
`vkey_engine.dll`, as a separate asset. It is NOT part of the AGPL-3.0 licensed
VKey application and is NOT covered by VKey's LICENSE.

If you obtained `vkey_engine.dll`, it is licensed under the PolyForm
Noncommercial License 1.0.0 and may be used for noncommercial purposes only:

    https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0

    Required Notice: Copyright 2026 PhatMT (https://github.com/phatMT97)

The noncommercial term is inherited, not chosen: the engine embeds a Vietnamese
syllable dictionary derived from a CC BY-NC corpus, so commercial rights in it
cannot be granted. The attribution that data requires:

  Vietnamese syllable dictionary and news frequencies
    Leipzig Corpora Collection, corpus `vie_news_2020_1M`. License: CC BY-NC.
    Dirk Goldhahn, Thomas Eckart, and Uwe Quasthoff. 2012. Building Large
    Monolingual Dictionaries at the Leipzig Corpora Collection: From 100 to 200
    Languages. In Proceedings of the Eighth International Conference on
    Language Resources and Evaluation (LREC'12), pages 759-765, Istanbul,
    Turkey. ELRA.  https://aclanthology.org/L12-1154/

  Spoken-register frequency evidence (frequencies only, never vocabulary)
    hermitdave/FrequencyWords, from OpenSubtitles2018 via OPUS.
    License: MIT, Copyright (c) 2016 Hermit Dave.
    Pierre Lison and Jorg Tiedemann. 2016. OpenSubtitles2016: Extracting Large
    Parallel Corpora from Movie and TV Subtitles. LREC'16.

  English word-membership list
    SCOWL (Spell Checking Oriented Word Lists) size tier 70, Kevin Atkinson.
    The collective work is Copyright 2000-2011 by Kevin Atkinson, whose
    permission notice requires that this copyright notice appear in all copies.
    Components reachable at size 70 that require attribution:
      WordNet 1.6 Copyright 1997 by Princeton University. All rights reserved.
      VARCON / Ispell word lists Copyright 1993, Geoff Kuenning, Granada Hills,
      CA. All rights reserved. Binary redistributions must reproduce that
      notice, its list of conditions and its disclaimer in the documentation or
